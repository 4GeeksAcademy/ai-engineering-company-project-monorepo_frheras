What we do

One logistics partner for the entire journey

From the moment an order is placed to the moment it is delivered or returned, TrackFlow takes care of the full operation for e-commerce brands.

Store

Inventory held in our warehouses in Los Angeles and Zaragoza, positioned to serve both sides of the Atlantic.

Pick & pack

Every day hundreds of orders are picked from the shelves, packed into boxes and handed off to carriers with care.

Ship

A network of 8 carriers across both countries — UPS, FedEx and DHL in the US; MRW, SEUR and DHL in Spain — so each parcel travels with the right partner.

Returns

Complete reverse logistics for the 18–25% of volume that comes back, with a dedicated team for every decision from approval to reconditioning.

Why TrackFlow

An experienced partner for growing e-commerce

E-commerce brands are great at making and selling products — getting them to customers' doors is where TrackFlow steps in.

  • Driving deliveries since 2009

    Founded in Los Angeles, now moving parcels from warehouse shelves to customer doorsteps for more than fifteen years.

  • Two countries, one partner

    Operate in the United States and Spain with warehouses in Los Angeles and Zaragoza — a single partner for both markets and their regulatory environments.

  • A network of 8 carriers

    UPS, FedEx and DHL in the United States; MRW, SEUR and DHL in Spain, plus two local carriers — flexibility in how every parcel reaches its destination.

  • Returns under control

    Reverse logistics for 18–25% of volume depending on client and country, with a dedicated team handling every approval, collection and reconditioning decision.

  • 130+ people on your operation

    A skilled operations team distributed between Los Angeles and Zaragoza, backed by a technology office in Spain that keeps systems running around the clock.

  • Built to run 24/7

    Customers don't stop expecting their parcels after 6pm. TrackFlow's operations are designed to keep moving when the business day ends.
