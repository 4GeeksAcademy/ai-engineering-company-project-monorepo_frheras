> Based exclusively on information from `CONTEXT.md`. Date: 2026-10-17.

---

## Mission

TrackFlow exists because e-commerce brands are good at making and selling products, but not at getting those products to customers' doors. TrackFlow handles the entire logistics operation for them: it stores their inventory, picks and packs orders, ships them through a network of carriers, and manages returns when products come back.

> _"For the brands that work with TrackFlow, the entire logistics operation — from the moment an order is placed to the moment it is delivered or returned — is TrackFlow's problem to solve."_

## Vision

The internal unit **TrackFlow Tech** (created by Daniel) has a clear mandate:

> _"Build the systems, integrations, and intelligent automations that allow TrackFlow to operate as the modern logistics company it needs to become."_

TrackFlow needs the infrastructure to run a two-country logistics operation at scale, with always-on systems (24/7), real-time data, and intelligent automation.

## Business Model

- **Type:** Last-mile delivery and warehouse management company.
- **Founded:** 2009 in Los Angeles, United States.
- **Markets:** United States and Spain.
- **Warehouses:** Los Angeles (USA) and Zaragoza (Spain).
- **Workforce:** ~130 employees.
- **Annual revenue:** ~9 million euros.
- **Clients:** E-commerce brands (B2B). End consumers (B2C) receive the packages.
- **Value proposition:** TrackFlow outsources all logistics for brands — warehousing, picking, packing, shipping, and returns — so they can focus on manufacturing and selling.

## Problems Solved

| Client problem                  | TrackFlow solution                                          |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Inventory and warehousing       | Stores stock in its own warehouses                          |
| Order preparation               | Picking and packing of orders                               |
| Shipping and distribution       | Network of 8 carriers (UPS, FedEx, DHL, MRW, SEUR, etc.)    |
| Package tracking                | Tracking and delivery management                            |
| Returns (reverse logistics)     | Reception, inspection, reconditioning, and disposal         |

## Current Company Situation

TrackFlow has **good clients, a skilled operations team, and a clear value proposition**. What it lacks is the infrastructure to run a two-country logistics business at scale:

- The two warehouses **cannot see each other's inventory**.
- Carrier performance data **does not exist in any structured form**.
- Returns are approved or rejected **one by one**.
- Customer queries are answered by agents consulting **a Word document on Google Drive**.
- The CEO **makes decisions based on a report assembled by hand**.

**Consequence:** TrackFlow is slower, more error-prone, and less profitable than it needs to be — and the gap is growing as competitors invest in automation.
